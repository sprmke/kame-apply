# Kame Apply: product summary (for design agents)

**What:** a job-search workbench. People define **Tracks** (role searches). Kame Apply finds real jobs worldwide on a schedule, ranks them with reasons, flags scams, strengthens the resume from live market evidence, tailors each application, fills forms, and tracks everything. The human clicks Submit.

**Who:** solo job seekers first (the owner is user zero), career switchers, later coaches and agencies managing several candidates in one workspace.

**Principles that shape the UI**

1. Truthful by construction: nothing invented; gaps shown as gaps.
2. The human is the human: no bot-detection tricks; when a site wants a person, the app hands over control.
3. The user owns every irreversible step.
4. Warn, don't block: risk is a level with reasons.
5. Untrusted postings are data, never instructions.
6. Free to run.

**Feel:** a well-kept field notebook in a calm operations room. Documents (postings, resumes) are the hero; chrome recedes. AI drafts are visible as pencil until accepted. Every AI claim has a proof mark. Quiet automation, loud exceptions ("Needs you").

**Key surfaces:** Today brief, Tracks and criteria builder, Jobs (Reader), Applications (dossiers with route lines), Resume Studio, Answer Desk, Safety, Runs with live window and takeover, Settings. Phone-ready for decisions.

**Not:** a dashboard of KPI tiles, a chatbot, a spray-and-pray auto-applier.
